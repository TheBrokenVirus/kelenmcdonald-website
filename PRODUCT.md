# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small business owners and independent creators (e.g. shop owners, studios, makers) who are shopping for a web designer/developer and want a site that reflects their specific brand rather than a generic template. Scope is intentionally broad — no narrower niche confirmed.

## Product Purpose

A one-person web design studio's own marketing/portfolio site. It exists to win freelance client work by demonstrating design and build quality, and to convert visitors into inquiries via the contact/email CTA.

## Positioning

Hand-built, bespoke sites as the alternative to template or page-builder sites ("no template with their logo swapped in," "no bloated templates, no page-builder plugins"). The differentiator is craft and intentionality, not price or speed.

## Operating Context

Solo practice — Kelen McDonald works alone; "Studio" in the name/brand is framing, not a literal team. Currently taking on a small number of new projects, working remotely with clients anywhere. Contact happens via a direct mailto CTA (hello@kelenmcdonald.com), with a stated ~couple-day follow-up turnaround.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services offered: brand identity (logo, color, type), web/interface design (wireframes through hi-fi), and hand-built development (no page-builder plugins/templates). Single static page today (`index.html`, no framework, no build step).

## Evidence on Hand

The three "Work" case studies (Marrow Coffee Roasters, Faraday Studio, Loom & Line) are confirmed **concept/speculative pieces** built to demonstrate range — not completed real-client engagements. They are correctly labeled "Concept" in the UI today; future work must preserve that honesty (not present them as real client outcomes, real testimonials, or real results) unless the user explicitly swaps in real project evidence later. No real client work, testimonials, press, or case-study data exists yet.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate craft directly through the site's own design and build quality — the site is itself a work sample.
- Never blur the line between speculative "Concept" work and real client work; label honestly.
- Keep the path to contact short and low-friction (single direct CTA to email).
- Solo-practice framing throughout — avoid copy or imagery that implies a larger team.
- Broad small-business/independent-creator audience — avoid narrowing to a specific vertical without explicit direction.
